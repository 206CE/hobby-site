1. Hunting

Opportunistic, active pursuit of high-value, mobile resources (but metaphorically any high-risk/high-reward target).
Involves tracking, chasing, risk, skill bursts, competition, thrill/adrenaline.
Examples: Actual hunting game; modern equivalents like sales chasing big deals, investing/trading for windfalls, entrepreneurship/startups, pursuing romantic/sexual partners aggressively, career advancement via bold moves, sports/competition, or even "hunting" for bargains/information online.

2. Gathering

Steady, reliable, lower-risk collection of abundant/renewable resources (plants, small foods, but broadly routine acquisition).
Involves patience, repetition, knowledge of locations/seasons, processing.
Examples: Foraging plants/nuts/honey; modern paid work (9-5 salary harvesting), shopping/grocery runs, routine chores (laundry, meal prep), saving money incrementally, networking for steady opportunities, farming/agriculture (post-hunter-gatherer extension).

3. Adapting

Monitoring change, learning, innovating, adjusting behaviors/strategies/tools to fit shifting conditions (environment, social, personal).
Involves observation, experimentation, knowledge transmission, flexibility/mobility.
Examples: Updating environmental knowledge around campfires; tool innovation/tinkering; deciding to move camp; learning new skills from others/kids playing at tasks; modern pivots (career changes, learning tech, migrating for jobs, therapy/self-improvement, cultural evolution, scientific discovery).

4. Protecting

Vigilance, defense, risk mitigation, maintaining safety for self/group/offspring.
Involves monitoring threats, deterrence, group strength, recovery from harm.
Examples: Watching for predators; fire maintenance; group sleeping arrangements; conflict resolution/diplomacy; healing/medicine; modern security (locks, laws, insurance, police/military, health care, emotional boundaries, avoiding danger).
